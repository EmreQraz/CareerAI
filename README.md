# CareerAI - AI-Powered Career Platform

**CareerAI** is an advanced SaaS platform designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and employers using artificial intelligence. It streamlines the career preparation journey by offering AI-driven CV optimization and interactive simulation tools.

---

## 🚀 Overview
Navigating the modern job market can be challenging. **CareerAI** aims to solve this by providing job seekers with automated, intelligent feedback on their application materials and practical preparation tools to build confidence before real-world interviews.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. AI-Driven CV Optimization
* **ATS Score Analysis:** Evaluates resumes against Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to ensure high parseability and keyword alignment.
* **Smart Content Recommendations:** Suggests impactful action verbs, quantifiable achievements, and professional formatting improvements tailored to specific job descriptions.

### 2. Interactive Interview Simulation
* **Role-Specific Mock Interviews:** Generates dynamic, context-aware interview questions based on the user's target role and industry.
* **Real-Time Feedback:** Analyzes response quality, pacing, and tone to provide actionable coaching.

---

## 📊 System Architecture & Product Strategy

* **Architecture Design:** Modular SaaS architecture built for scalability, utilizing RESTful API endpoints for seamless frontend-backend communication.
* **Database Schema:** Entity-Relationship (ER) models designed to handle secure user profiles, resume versioning, analytics logs, and subscription tiers.
* **Monetization & Business Model:** A tiered SaaS subscription model (Freemium, Pro, and Enterprise) targeting individuals, bootcamps, and career coaching services.
* **Market Analysis:** Comprehensive Total Addressable Market (TAM), Serviceable Available Market (SAM), and Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) calculations tailored to the HR-Tech sector.
